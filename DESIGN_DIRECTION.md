# Design direction

**Idea:** "A country house journal." Parma is presented the way a private estate would print its own book: large, still landscape plates; a frontispiece hero; a serif set big and quiet; small sans marginalia. The Blue Ridge autumn photography is the colour source, and the interiors (brass, brocade, candlelight) supply the accent.

## The one bold move
The hero is a frontispiece. The autumn vineyard plate runs full-bleed, and "Parma" is set in Cormorant at very large size, bottom-left, with the place line beneath. Everything else stays disciplined.

## Reviewed against the defaults (and revised)
- **Tracked-caps eyebrow labels:** cut, replaced by sentence-case sans in the muted colour.
- **Cream plus terracotta:** the ground is a greyer parchment (#F2EDE4), and the accent is brass taken from the logo, used only on lines and focus.
- **"01 / 02 / 03" on the four worlds:** removed, since the worlds are not a sequence. Suite numbers are not shown either.
- **Scroll reveal on every section:** limited to one hero moment.
